# Product

## Register

product

## Users

College librarians and library staff managing a shared catalog, member directory, and book-loan workflow. They need fast, dependable information during routine circulation work, often while assisting people in person.

## Product Purpose

ShelfLife centralizes catalog search, live availability, member records, and issue or return workflows for a college library. Success means staff can confidently complete a common task without losing their place or risking an incorrect loan.

## Brand Personality

Warm, welcoming, and academically confident. The interface should feel like a well-kept contemporary library rather than a cold administrative database.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic dark SaaS dashboards, loud gradients, decorative glass panels, cramped data-entry forms, and interfaces that make the operational workflow feel intimidating.

## Design Principles

1. Make the next library task immediately clear.
2. Pair operational precision with a calm, human atmosphere.
3. Keep status and stock information easy to scan at a glance.
4. Reveal complexity only when a librarian needs it.
5. Make controls reliable, accessible, and forgiving of small mistakes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG AA contrast and keyboard-access expectations. Provide visible focus states, understandable labels, and meaningful non-color status cues.
